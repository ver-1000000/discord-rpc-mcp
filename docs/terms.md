# Terms of Use

discord-rpc-mcp connects your MCP client or AI agent to Discord on your computer.
It reads Discord information and, when enabled, performs actions through Discord's RPC interface.
This is an independent project, not affiliated with Discord.

Use it only with accounts and information you are authorized to access, and comply with applicable law and Discord's terms and policies.
Keep credentials private and review the data practices of your connected MCP client and AI services.
Discord prohibits using API message content to train AI models without its express permission.
Do not use this application with an agent or AI service unless you can prevent that content from being used for training.
